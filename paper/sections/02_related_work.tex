\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\subsection{Spine MRI Grading}
% Windsor SpineNetV2, Ningshen rsna-2024 Kaggle pipeline, RSNA top-10
% solutions, SPIDER benchmark. Position our contribution: 3D
% volumetric grading with cross-dataset zero-shot label extension.
SpineNetV2~\cite{windsor2024spinenet} provides a 3D ResNet-34 backbone
pretrained on lumbar IVD volumes, which we adopt as our base. The
RSNA~2024 challenge produced multiple 2D pipelines~\cite{nshen7rsna2024};
these treat each IVD as a 2D crop and train independent models per
condition. Our approach differs in two ways: (i) we use volumetric
input throughout, and (ii) we add a label-space-extensible head
allowing inference on unseen disease classes from the SPIDER
dataset~\cite{spider} without any SPIDER training.

\subsection{Attention for Imbalanced Medical Imaging}
% CBAM (Woo 2018), SE-Net (Hu 2018), small-lesion attention precedents.
CBAM~\cite{woo2018cbam} combines channel and spatial attention at near-zero
parameter cost. We choose CBAM over SE-Net~\cite{hu2018senet} because the
spatial gate is the critical component for localising small Severe
lesions; non-local~\cite{wang2018nonlocal} and transformer self-attention
scale poorly under our small-data regime.

\subsection{Medical Vision-Language Foundation Models}
% BiomedCLIP, MedGemma, RadFM, RAD-DINO. Justify why a frozen
% contrastive encoder is the right choice for our task.
Recent medical foundation models include BiomedCLIP~\cite{zhang2023biomedclip},
RadFM~\cite{wu2025radfm}, RAD-DINO~\cite{perezgarcia2024raddino} and
MedGemma~\cite{sebro2025medgemma}. Of these, BiomedCLIP is the only
contrastive image–text encoder small enough (${\sim}86$M image params)
to integrate as a frozen feature extractor on a consumer GPU while
exposing a CLIP-style~\cite{radford2021clip} embedding space suitable
for cosine-similarity zero-shot classification. MedGemma and RadFM are
generative models whose multimodal arms are 2D-only and whose
fine-tuning requirements exceed our resource regime; we leave
plug-in replacement of BiomedCLIP with their contrastive companions
(e.g.\ MedSigLIP) as future work.
