\textbf{Contributions.} We address three coupled problems in lumbar
spine MRI grading with a single hybrid pipeline:
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Imbalance handling.} A combined toolkit of Focal Loss,
        square-root class weights, minority oversampling, and
        augmentation lifts mean Severe-class recall from $11.9\%$ to
        $49.6\%$ (a $4.2\times$ gain) on RSNA's three stenosis
        conditions, with mean F1 macro improving in absolute terms
        despite the deliberate accuracy trade-off.
  \item \textbf{Attention-driven feature quality.} CBAM (channel and
        spatial attention) on a 3D ResNet-34 backbone recovers
        foraminal Severe F1 from $0.000$ (baseline) to
        $0.276/0.283$ on left/right foraminal classes, where the
        baseline collapses entirely on the minority class.
  \item \textbf{Label-space extension.} Fusing the CBAM backbone
        with a frozen BiomedCLIP image-text encoder enables
        \emph{both} (i) zero-shot prediction over $8$ unseen SPIDER
        labels via cosine-similarity prompting (Mean F1 macro
        $= 0.362$, no SPIDER-specific training) and (ii) supervised
        SPIDER transfer with ${\sim}5$M trainable parameters
        (Mean F1 macro $= 0.623$). We further report a
        \emph{selective transfer} pattern, characterising which
        SPIDER labels benefit from RSNA-learned features.
\end{itemize}
